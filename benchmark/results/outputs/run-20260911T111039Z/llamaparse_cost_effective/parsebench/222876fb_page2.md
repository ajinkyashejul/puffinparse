# LTC2228/LTC2227/LTC2226

## **ABSOLUTE MAXIMUM RATINGS**

**0V<sub>DD</sub> = V<sub>DD</sub> (Notes 1, 2)**
Supply Voltage (V<sub>DD</sub>) .................................................... 4V
Digital Output Ground Voltage (OGND) ....... -0.3V to 1V
Analog Input Voltage (Note 3) ....... -0.3V to (V<sub>DD</sub> + 0.3V)
Digital Input Voltage ............................ -0.3V to (V<sub>DD</sub> + 0.3V)
Digital Output Voltage ........................ -0.3V to (OV<sub>DD</sub> + 0.3V)
Power Dissipation .................................................... 1500mW
Operating Temperature Range
LTC2228C, LTC2227C, LTC2226C ............ 0°C to 70°C
LTC2228I, LTC2227I, LTC2226I ............ -40°C to 85°C
Storage Temperature Range ........................ -65°C to 125°C
