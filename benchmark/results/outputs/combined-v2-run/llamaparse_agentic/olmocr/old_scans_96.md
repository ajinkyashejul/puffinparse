Camp near Falmouth. Va
Head. quarters Genl Sturges (2<sup>nd</sup>) Division
Dec. 8<sup>th</sup> 1862. —

Messrs Brown & Co. —

Dear friends
Mr Wells
return tomorrow, and I improve the op-
portunity to send a line by him to you,
not feeling quite certain if posted matter
reaches directly when sent from the Army. —
We reached Aquia Creek safely in
the time anticipated, and to my great joy
learned immediately that our old friend
Capt (Maj.) Hall (of the 21<sup>st</sup>) was Q. Master
as soon as the Boat was unloaded he came
on board and spent the remainder of the evening
with me, — we had a home chat I assure you
— remained till the next day, sent a bbl of
apples etc up to the Capt's quarters, and
proceeded with the remainder of our luggage
for which it is needless to say ready transportation
