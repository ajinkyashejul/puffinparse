Acquired in-process research and development (“IPR&D”) consists of existing research and development projects at the time of the acquisition. Projects that
qualify as IPR&D assets represent those that have not yet reached technological feasibility and have no alternative future use. After initial recognition,
acquired IPR&D assets are accounted for as indefinite-lived intangible assets. Development costs incurred after acquisition on acquired development projects
are expensed as incurred. Upon completion of development, acquired IPR&D assets are considered amortizable intangible assets. If the IPR&D project is
abandoned, the Company writes off the related purchased intangible asset in the period it is abandoned.
