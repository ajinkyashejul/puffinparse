# Corrigendum to “Midwifery continuity of carer in an area of high socio- economic disadvantage in London: A retrospective analysis of Albany Midwifery Practice outcomes using routine data (1997–2009)” [Midwifery 48 (2017) 1– 10]

The authors regret that on page 8 we inadvertently referred to NICE as providing policy and policy direction. This was incorrect. NICE provides national clinical guidance based on systematic reviews. The sentence should read: A large study comparing perinatal outcomes by planned place of birth (Birthplace in England Collaborative Group, 2011) supported the clinical guidance to off er women with low risk pregnancies a choice of birth settings, including birth at home (NICE, 2014).

The authors would like to apologise for any inconvenience caused.
