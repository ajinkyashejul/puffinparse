## Figure 1.6. Alien temporary work permits, Thailand

Monthly bar chart of alien temporary work permits in Thailand from January 2019 to January 2022. Permit counts remain near 115,000–120,000 through much of 2019 and early 2020, peak just above 120,000 around January 2020, and then decline to roughly 93,000–95,000 by late 2021 and early 2022.

Source: Department of Employment, Thailand (2022)

## Figure 1.7. Non-citizen population in Malaysia (in thousands)

Bar chart titled “Non-citizen population in Malaysia (in thousands)” showing annual values from 2016 to 2021: 3,230, 3,288, 3,323, 3,140, 2,907, and 2,693, with a peak in 2018 and a decline through 2021.

Source: Department of Statistics, Malaysia (2022). Figure for 2021 is an estimate.

## Figure 1.8. Singapore foreign workforce stock (in thousands)

Bar chart of Singapore foreign workforce stock in thousands, showing annual December values from 2016 to 2021: 1,393, 1,368, 1,386, 1,427, 1,232, and 1,200.

Source: Compilation by Manpower Research & Statistics Department (Ministry of Manpower, Singapore, 2022).
