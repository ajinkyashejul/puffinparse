EXPERIENCE CREDIT RULE
1. Eligibility
a. When the premium at the inception date is $1,500 or more excluding Equipment Breakdown and Employment
Practices Liability, prior to the application of a Scheduled Premium Modification Factor, an Experience Credit for a
favorable loss ratio shall apply to that premium. Losses used to calculate the loss ratio are to include loss and loss
adjusting expense reserves on open claims, and allocated loss adjusting expenses, but not Equipment Breakdown -
Boiler and Machinery Coverage losses.
b. This credit shall not be applied as a new credit during the policy term.
c. When the credit applies at the inception, any changes occurring during the policy period will reflect this credit.

2. New Business

Documented experience must be obtained from the prior carrier for this credit to apply. This experience must include not
less than the thirty (30) consecutive month period that begins thirty six (36) months prior to the proposed inception date.
