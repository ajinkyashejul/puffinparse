<b>The White House has taken the first official steps toward rewriting regulations for the National Environmental
Policy Act.</b>

The Council on Environmental Quality submitted an <u><b>advanced notice of proposed rulemaking</b></u> on NEPA to
the <i>Federal Register</i> last Friday. A CEQ spokesman said the document would be published "in the next couple
of days."
