<b>Figure 1.6.</b> Alien temporary work permits, Thailand

Monthly bar chart of alien temporary work permits in Thailand from January 2019 to January 2022. Permits remain around 110,000–120,000 through early 2020, peak near 120,000 in January 2020, then decline steadily to about 90,000 by January 2022.

<b>Source:</b> Department of Employment, Thailand (2022)

<b>Figure 1.7.</b> Non-citizen population in Malaysia (in thousands)

Bar chart of Malaysia’s non-citizen population from 2016 to 2021, measured in thousands. The population rises from 3,230 in 2016 to a peak of 3,323 in 2018, then declines each year to 3,140 in 2019, 2,907 in 2020, and 2,693 in 2021.

<b>Source:</b> Department of Statistics, Malaysia (2022). Figure for 2021 is an estimate.

<b>Figure 1.8.</b> Singapore foreign workforce stock (in thousands)

Bar chart of Singapore’s foreign workforce stock from December 2016 to December 2021, measured in thousands. Stock declines from 1,393 to 1,368 in 2017, rises to 1,386 in 2018, and peaks at 1,427 in 2019; it then falls to 1,232 in 2020 and 1,200 in 2021.

<b>Source:</b> Compilation by Manpower Research & Statistics Department (Ministry of Manpower,
Singapore, 2022).
